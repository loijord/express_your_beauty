# express_your_beauty (kūnas yra tavo namai)
This is a development site of my project `express your beauty` written in Jupyter Notebook.

For production use, go to [https://loijord.github.io/express_your_beauty/](https://loijord.github.io/express_your_beauty/)

Are you interested in my body expression, especially in a beauty dance, dress and make-up? So come and follow this vogueing pretty in some club deep in a city. You'll find there are two sex-independent genres of street dance

* Vogue originated in the 1980s in NewYork
* Waacking originated in the 1970s in Los Angeles

These two began to emerge in Lithuania, in the early 2010s. Special thanks to Raidas and Brigida for bringing it there and growing a few families of dancers. I dedicate this site for evolution of Waacking and Vogue genres in Vilnius after 2010.
